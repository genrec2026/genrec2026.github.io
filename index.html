About

A workshop on generative models for search and recommendation systems.

Search and recommendation systems are being reshaped by generative models that can understand intent, synthesize candidates, reason over catalogs, and personalize experiences across modalities.

This workshop will bring together researchers and practitioners working on generative retrieval, recommendation, ranking, evaluation, user modeling, multimodal discovery, agentic shopping and content experiences, and the infrastructure required to serve these systems at scale.

The program will feature invited talks, discussions, and community exchange around the technical and scientific foundations of generative search and recommendations at NeurIPS 2026.
