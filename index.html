About

A workshop on generative models for search and recommendation systems.

Generative AI is fundamentally changing how people discover information, products, content, and ideas. Search and recommendation systems are among the largest real-world applications of machine learning. As they are moving beyond retrieve-and-rank pipelines and toward systems that can reason about intent, it is becoming more and more relevant to discuss the future of systems that generate candidates, synthesize information, and adapt dynamically to users. This shift raises fundamental questions at the intersection of foundation models, information retrieval, recommender systems, and learning from user interactions.

The Generative Search + Recommendations workshop will bring together leading experts from academia and industry to explore these emerging paradigms and their implications for machine learning. Through invited talks, speakers will share perspectives on generative retrieval and ranking, LLM-based candidate generation, personalization and user modeling, multimodal discovery, conversational and agentic systems, and generative interfaces.

The workshop highlights open challenges that are central to the NeurIPS community: how to train and evaluate these systems, learn from evolving user behavior, balance relevance with exploration and diversity, ensure reliability and alignment, and build foundation-model-powered systems that operate effectively at massive scale.

We aim to spark discussion about the next generation of intelligent discovery systems—and the machine learning foundations needed to build them.
